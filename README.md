### 🙋‍♂️ About Me

Embedded systems and firmware engineer with hands-on experience across the full hardware-software stack — STM32 bare-metal HAL, ESP32 ESP-IDF, FreeRTOS multitasking, and hardware bring-up across UART, SPI, I2C, and PWM. Built and deployed 8+ end-to-end systems spanning real-time firmware, autonomous robotics, UAV platforms, and long-range IoT. National hackathon winner (Hackwave'25, 150+ teams). Three-time ARISS Special Student Achievement Award recipient for receiving SSTV transmissions from the ISS. Licensed HAM radio operator (VU2MZS) with 100+ worldwide contacts across analog, digital, and SSTV modes.
